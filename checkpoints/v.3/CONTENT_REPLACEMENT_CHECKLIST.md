# Content replacement checklist

The supplied media is now represented by optimized derivatives in the site, and public portfolio permission was confirmed on 02 November 2026. Use one row per final project to finish the public credits. Do not remove the development labels until every required field for that project is complete.

## Required project fields

- Public title and type: Video or Photography
- Exact role: Director, Camera, Photographer, Editor, Concept, or another verified credit
- Year and location
- One-sentence context and 40–80 word description
- Full collaborator and client/artist credits
- Confirm the existing public-use authorization remains applicable if a project is materially recut
- Optimized cover/poster with meaningful alt text
- Optional video destination or web-ready video file, tested without autoplay audio

## AI / Generative Media

- Six AI-generated poster studies are supplied and integrated as a temporary series
- Keep AI work in the dedicated **AI / Generative** section rather than Photography
- Disclose the generative process on every project and identify any source photography or client constraints
- Confirm the tools, models, and source assets may be used and published commercially
- Add final titles, years, context, roles, and meaningful alt text

## Media preparation

- Cover: AVIF/WebP preferred, approximately 1800–2400 px on the long edge
- Responsive alternatives: at least 800 px and 1400 px versions for major images
- Video poster: match the source video aspect ratio
- Video: H.264 MP4 plus WebM when useful; avoid committing raw masters
- Filenames: lowercase, descriptive, hyphenated, and stable
- Keep a private source ledger outside the public repository with rights status and source filenames
